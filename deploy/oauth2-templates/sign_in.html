What's different

Not another dashboard you have to set up.

Jira already has the data. Jira Pulse turns it into answers, the way our team actually works.

💬

Just ask, in your words

No JQL, no filters. Type in English or Hindi, or tap the mic and speak in English. Follow-up questions just work.

🧭

Knows how we use Jira

Features are components, sprints are “Q4 FY26 - Sprint 4”, and our workflow runs To Do to Released. It speaks our language out of the box.

🎯

Numbers you can trust

Every count comes straight from live Jira, with links to the exact tickets. If it can't find something, it says so instead of guessing.

👤

Knows who you are

Sign in with your company Google account and “my tasks” means yours, with no setup and no picking your name from a list.

⚡

Spots problems for you

Stuck queues, high-priority bugs, people who look busy on paper but are quiet in Jira, carried-over work: flagged before stand-up.

📏

Keeps standards honest

Checks every ticket title against our naming standard and every worklog for a real description, and suggests the fix.
